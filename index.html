01 — The mission

Being online has never been easier. Feeling connected still is not.

Most apps are built to hold your attention. ORVEX is built to hold people together. We make it simple to find the right few people — classmates, neighbors, teammates, volunteers — and actually do something meaningful with them.

Instead of endless feeds, ORVEX forms small orbits: focused circles built around what people need and what they can offer. Study help. A listening ear. A weekend cleanup. A creative project. Every orbit exists to make someone's day a little lighter.

02 — Features

Everything a community needs, nothing it doesn't.

◎

Live Spaces

Real-time chat rooms for studying, talking, volunteering, creating, gaming and building. Messages reach everyone online instantly.

✦

Orbit Matching

Pick your interests and ORVEX shows who is online right now with the same ones, so you can say hi in one click.

❖

Give-Back Board

Ask for help or offer yours. Tap "I can help" and a private chat opens with the person who posted.

◐

Private Messages

Talk one-on-one with anyone you connect with. Chats are saved, so you can pick up where you left off.

⌘

Accessible by Design

Works on phones and laptops, keyboard friendly, high contrast and reduced-motion aware.

☄

Safety Tools

No email or phone number needed. Report any message, rate limits stop spam, and sensitive rooms show help reminders.
